The Venture

ManaProof is a data-driven Magic: The Gathering venture. It applies data-science techniques to identify viable Commander decks from card collections, assembles them physically, and sells them as ready-to-play products — making Commander accessible to more players.

> ./view --data-science

Card collections are analyzed with techniques from data science and information theory to surface non-obvious card-commander synergies and build coherent, playable decks.

The result is decks that play tighter than randomly assembled piles — built from undervalued cards, sold at accessible prices.

> ./view --pipeline

  • 1. Inventory Analysis Load and normalize card collections for consistent matching across the pipeline.
  • 2. Deck Discovery Cross-reference available cards against Commander deck data to identify which decks can be built from the collection.
  • 3. Optimization Complete partial decks with synergistic additions, flagging what's needed vs. what's already owned.
  • 4. Extraction & Assembly Generate physical pick lists with conflict resolution — when two decks compete for the same card, the system picks the best assignment.
  • 5. Catalog & Pricing Output ready-to-import deck lists with pricing data for the storefront.
